# Help Center and Ask School AI guide: status

Generated by hand at the end of the build; numbers come from `npm run help:check`. Re-run it for current figures.

## Delivered

- **Help Center** at `/help` (also `/guide`): search, browse by module and by role, role-aware article lists, feedback, "Open this screen", a link to Ask School AI, optional **Walk me through it** tours (3), and a platform-administrator panel to publish the guide to Ask School AI.
- **67 articles across all 39 modules**, written from the code and the running app. Status: all `reviewed` (checked against code), none `verified`.
- **PDF manual** built from the same Markdown (`npm run help:pdf`), public edition committed to `public/help/`; admin and platform editions go to `dist-docs/` and are handed out by hand.
- **Ask School AI**: deterministic answers and typed, allow-listed navigation in the web app; keyword search over the guide in AiService (`search_help`, `open_screen`), permission filtering on the server, publish restricted to the platform administrator. No paid model or embedding service is needed for the guide.
- **Tooling**: inventory, coverage and traceability reports, PDF freshness check, AI index export, screenshot capture with synthetic data, and a live label audit.
- **Tests**: help, registry, assistant, walkthrough and admin-panel tests in SMS UI; 18 help tests in AiService (220 total pass).

## Known limitations

- Coverage is not complete: about 50 of 87 screens have an article of their own, and the action figure (about 63%) is a heuristic that only finds labels no article mentions.
- The label audit checks labels, not that steps work; states that need data (row actions, toasts, conditional fields) are not reached.
- Walkthrough cards are not reachable by keyboard or screen reader while a form drawer is open, because the drawer hides the page behind it.
- Parents and students are only offered tasks whose article lists their role; staff roles follow the module setting (see the README).
- The assistant treats a single common word as a question for the AI service, not as a help request.
- Communication Center: only in-app delivery works; the guide says so.
- The seeded demo admin saw "No transport access", which may be a missing permission backfill unrelated to the guide.
