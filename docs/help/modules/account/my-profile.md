**My profile** shows the record the school holds for you (a student, parent, teacher or staff member) and lets you ask for corrections. Open it from the **Account menu**.

## See your details

The page is organised in sections such as **Overview**, **Attendance**, **Documents**, **Guardian & Emergency**, **Medical**, **ID Card**, **Performance**, **Qualifications & Experience**, **Salary** and **Transport & Hostel**, depending on who you are. A parent with more than one child picks **Whose profile** to look at. If you see "No profile found", no profile is linked to your account: ask the school's office to check it.

A banner can say **Still needed** with the fields that are **missing**; filling them in is how the school keeps your record complete.

## Change your details

1. Select **Edit profile** (or **Update my details**).
2. Change the fields you need. Fields marked as protected cannot be edited.
3. Select **Send for approval**.

Most changes go to your approver ("Sent to {{who}} for approval"). Some are **Applied straight away**, and changing a name or date of birth is **Approved by the school's office**. Until it is approved, your profile keeps the current values ("Your profile keeps the current values until it is approved.").

## Follow your request

- While it waits you see **Change waiting for approval** or "Waiting for ... to approve". Select **Withdraw request** if you made a mistake ("Request withdrawn.").
- Under **Recent requests** you can open **View details** for what was **Requested** and what happened.
- If it comes back ("The school's office asked for changes" or "Your last change was rejected"), your profile was not changed. Edit it again to send a corrected request.
