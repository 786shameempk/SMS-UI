Every person at your school signs in with their own **email address** and **password**. What you can open afterwards depends on your role and on the modules your school has switched on, so two people at the same school can see different menus.

## What happens after you sign in

- You always land on the **Dashboard**, not on the page you used last.
- The menu on the left (the menu button on a phone) lists only the modules available to you.
- Your name and role appear at the top right. Select your avatar to open the **Account menu**.

## The Account menu

| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Security settings** | Change your password, review where you are signed in and remove trusted devices. See [Change your password and manage sessions](help:account-security-settings). |
| **Log out** | Ends this session and returns you to the sign-in page. |

## Sessions and timing out

A session ends when you choose **Log out**, when the time your school allows has passed, or when you sign out of a device from [Security settings](route:account.security). When a session runs out, you return to the sign-in page with the message "Your session has expired. Please sign in again to continue where you left off."

> **Tip:** Leave **Remember me** ticked on your own computer so you stay signed in for longer. Untick it on a shared computer.

## Mobile apps

The parent and student app, the teacher app and the staff app use the same email and password as the website.
