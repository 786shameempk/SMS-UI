## Open Security settings

1. Select your avatar at the top right to open the **Account menu**.
2. Choose **Security settings**. The page is headed **Security**.

The page has three tabs: **Password**, **Sessions** and **Devices**.

## Change your password

1. Open the **Password** tab (**Change password**).
2. Type your **Current password**.
3. Type your **New password** (at least 8 characters) and type it again in **Confirm new password**.
4. Select **Update password**. A confirmation message appears and the form clears.

| Field | Message when wrong |
|---|---|
| **Current password** | "Current password is required" |
| **New password** | "Must be at least 8 characters" |
| **Confirm new password** | "Please confirm your new password" or "Passwords do not match" |

If the current password is wrong or the change is refused, the error appears as a message at the top of the screen and nothing changes.

## See where you are signed in

Open the **Sessions** tab (**Active sessions**). Each row is a browser or device where you are signed in. Select **Sign out** on a row to end that session; the message "Signed out on" followed by the device name appears.

## Trusted devices

Open the **Devices** tab (**Trusted devices**) to see devices that can skip two-factor verification, with the system and the time each was last used. Select **Remove** on a device to stop trusting it.
