| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Enter your email address" or "That doesn't look like a valid email" | Type the full email address your school gave you, for example name@yourschool.edu. |
| "Enter your password" | The password field is empty. |
| A message above the form saying the sign-in failed | Check the email and password. Passwords are case sensitive. If you are unsure of the password, use [Forgot password](help:account-forgot-password). |
| "Your session has expired. Please sign in again to continue where you left off." | Sign in again. Sessions end after the time your school allows, or if you were signed out from another device. |
| You are sent to **Set a new password** every time | Your account still has a temporary password. Follow [Set a new password after a temporary one](help:account-temporary-password). |
| The reset email does not arrive | It can take a few minutes. Check spam or junk folders, make sure you used the email address registered with your school, then ask your administrator. |
| "This link is invalid or incomplete" | The reset link is old or was cut off. Request a new one from **Forgot password**. |
| Sign in with Google does nothing useful | Google sign-in is not enabled for your school. Use your email and password. |
| The one-time code does not arrive, or "We couldn't send a code" | Wait for the **Resend code** timer and try again. Mobile codes go only to a **verified** number: see [Verify your mobile number](help:account-verify-mobile-number). Email codes go to your account email; check spam. If **Mobile OTP** / **Email OTP** are not shown at all, your school has not turned them on. See [Sign in with a one-time code](help:account-sign-in-with-code). |
| "We couldn't check that code" or the code is refused | Codes expire after a few minutes and five wrong tries cancel a code. Request a new one. |
| You can sign in but a menu item is missing | Your role or your school's plan does not include that module. Ask your school administrator. |

> **Tip:** If you share the computer with others, untick **Remember me** and always use **Log out** from the Account menu when you finish.
