When an administrator creates your account or resets your password, you may receive a **temporary password**. You can sign in with it, but you must choose your own password before you can use anything else.

## Steps

1. Sign in with your email and the temporary password.
2. The page **Set a new password** opens. It says you signed in with a temporary password.
3. Type the temporary password in **Temporary password**.
4. Type your choice in **New password** (at least 8 characters) and again in **Confirm new password**.
5. Select **Update password**. The message "Password updated. Sign in with your new password." appears.
6. Sign in again with the new password.

## Checks

| Field | Message when wrong |
|---|---|
| **Temporary password** | "Enter the temporary password you were given" |
| **New password** | "Must be at least 8 characters" |
| **Confirm new password** | "Please confirm your new password" or "Passwords do not match" |

You can leave this page without changing the password by selecting **Sign out** on it. You return to the sign-in page, and every other page sends you back here until you change the password.
