**AI Features** collects the school's AI tools on one page. You see only the tools your role and your school's settings allow, so the list differs from person to person.

## The tools

![The AI Features page with an Overview, a Recommended for you row and tools grouped under Ask & learn, Teach & create and Insights.](shot:ai-features-desktop "The AI Features page.")

The page opens on an **Overview** of cards, with a **Recommended for you** row at the top. Tools are grouped as follows.

| Group | Tool | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| Ask & learn | **Ask School AI** | Ask about attendance, homework, results and fees. |
| Ask & learn | **Study Assistant** | Study from school materials, with page citations. |
| Ask & learn | **My Learning** | Your strengths and what to practise next. |
| Teach & create | **Teacher Tools** | Generate questions and teaching material. |
| Teach & create | **Study Materials** | Upload documents the Study Assistant learns from. |
| Teach & create | **Content Assistant** | Draft report-card comments, reminders and notes. |
| Teach & create | **Review & Publish** | Check AI-written content before it goes out. |
| Insights | **Insights** | Findings computed from this school's real data. |
| Insights | **At-Risk Students** | Low attendance, weak results or overdue fees. |
| Insights | **Analytics Assistant** | Ask questions about trends in your data. |
| Manage | **AI Usage** | How much AI the school is using. |

## Ask School AI from anywhere

The floating **Ask School AI** button opens the assistant in a side panel without leaving the page you are on. It can also answer questions about using School Sphere itself; see [Ask School AI](help:help-ask-school-ai).

> **Note:** AI-written drafts, such as report-card comments, should be read before they are sent. Use **Review & Publish** where your school has it.
