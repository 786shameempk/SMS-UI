**Performance & Appraisal** runs staff and teacher appraisals with 360-degree feedback. HR or the principal opens a **cycle**, picks the people being appraised, and the system asks managers, peers, the person themselves and (for teachers) students and parents to answer a short review form. Scores are combined, approved and finalized, and the employee sees the released result.

## What you see depends on your role

The tabs at the top of the module are shown only if you can use them.

| Tab | Who it is for |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | People who manage cycles: progress, overdue reviews, score distribution. |
| **Pending reviews** | Anyone who has been asked to review someone. See [Complete a review](help:appraisal-complete-review). |
| **My appraisals** | Anyone who is being appraised. See [See your appraisal result](help:appraisal-my-results). |
| **Appraisal cycles** | People who manage cycles. See [Run an appraisal cycle](help:appraisal-run-cycle). |
| **Criteria & questions** | People who manage the question bank and review templates. |
| **Configuration** | Rating scale, reviewer groups and weights, approval steps, privacy and retention. See [Set up appraisals](help:appraisal-configure). |
| **Results & reports** | Filterable reports with **Download CSV** and **Print**. |
| **History** | Past cycles and the audit trail. |

Students and parents see only **Teacher Feedback** in the sidebar. See [Give feedback on a teacher](help:appraisal-teacher-feedback).

## Privacy

- Anonymous reviewers are never named. Groups with too few anonymous answers are not shown at all ("Hidden (too few responses)"), so nobody can be singled out.
- Notifications are generic: they never contain scores, comments or reviewer names.
- Confidential questions can be put to staff reviewers only, never to students or parents.
- The audit trail records who did what and when, never scores, comments or reviewer names.
