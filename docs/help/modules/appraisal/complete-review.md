When an appraisal or feedback review is assigned to you, it appears on **Pending reviews**. If nothing is waiting you see "No reviews waiting for you".

## Steps

1. Open **Performance & Appraisal** and choose **Pending reviews**.
2. Find the review. Each row shows the due date ("Due …"), **Overdue** if it is late, or "No deadline". Select **Start review**, or **Continue** if you saved a draft.
3. Answer the questions one section at a time ("Section 1 of 4"). The **Progress** bar shows how far you are. Questions are rating scales, yes/no, or multiple choice; many have an **Optional comment**. Choose **Not applicable** where you cannot judge.
4. Select **Next** and **Back** to move between sections. Select **Save draft** at any time; "Draft saved" appears and you can return later.
5. When every required question is answered, select **Submit review**. If some are missing you see "Some questions still need an answer."

"Review submitted" and "Thank you - your review was submitted" confirm it was recorded. A submitted review cannot be edited: "This review can no longer be edited." You can still find it with **Show submitted reviews**.

> **Note:** If a review shows "This review can't be opened", it may not be assigned to you or it is not open yet. Check the cycle's start date with the person who runs it.

A review marked **Anonymous** is not attributed to you in what the employee or manager sees.
