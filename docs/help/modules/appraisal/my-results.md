1. Open **Performance & Appraisal** and choose **My appraisals**.
2. Each row is a cycle you are part of. A cycle that is not finished shows "Your result will appear here once it is finalized and released."
3. When the result is released, the row shows "Result released" and "Overall score …". Select **Open** to read the details.

The result page shows your score, **Feedback by reviewer group** and your **Development goals**. Whether you can also read the written comments is a school setting ("Employees can also read the written feedback"), and comments the school has hidden are not shown.

If you see "No appraisals yet", you have not been added to a cycle.
