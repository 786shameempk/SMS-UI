1. Choose **Teacher Feedback** in the sidebar.
2. Select the feedback request. Parents may get one per child, subject or teacher depending on the school's settings.
3. Answer the questions (ratings, yes/no or choices) and add an **Optional comment** if you like.
4. Select **Save draft** to finish later, or **Submit review**. "Your feedback has been recorded. Thank you." confirms it.

Your answers are anonymous: teachers and managers see only combined results, and small groups are hidden so no one can be identified. Feedback already submitted cannot be edited.
