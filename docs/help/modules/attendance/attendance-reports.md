Reports use the attendance recorded in [Mark student attendance](help:attendance-mark-students).

1. Open **Attendance** and select the **Reports** tab.
2. Choose **Daily**, **Monthly** or **Yearly**.

## Daily

**Daily attendance** shows one row for every class and section on the date you pick, with **Roster**, **Present**, **Absent**, **Late**, **Half Day**, **Leave** and **% Present**.

## Monthly

**Monthly attendance** is for one section. Choose the **Class**, the **Section** and the **Month**. The grid has a row per **Student** and a column for each day, ending in **% Present**.

## Yearly

**Yearly attendance trend** shows the percentage present by month across the recorded attendance history, for the section you choose.
