The calendar brings the school's dates together: holidays, exams, the academic calendar, homework due dates, staff leave, birthdays and online classes.

## Steps

1. Open **Calendar** from the menu. The month is shown in the card heading.
2. Use **Previous month** and **Next month** to move around.
3. Select a day. The card on the side lists what is on it, or says "No events on this day." Coloured dots on a day show which kinds of events it has. Events that last several days, such as holidays and staff leave, appear on every day they cover.
4. In the **Show** card, tick or untick a kind of event to show or hide it.

The event kinds are **Holiday**, **Exam**, **Academic calendar**, **Event**, **Homework due**, **Staff on leave**, **Birthday** and **Online classes**.

> **Note:** This screen only shows events; it has no button for adding them. Holidays and academic dates are added in Academic Setup, exams in Examinations, and online classes in Online Classes.
