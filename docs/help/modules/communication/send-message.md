Use this to message parents, students, staff or groups.

## Steps

1. Open **Communication Center** from the menu. The page is headed **Communication center**; stay on the **Compose** tab.
2. Optionally choose **Start from a template**.
3. Add a **Subject** if the message goes by email, then write the **Message**.
4. Under **Channels**, choose at least one: **Email**, **SMS**, **Push**, **WhatsApp** or **In-app**.
5. Under **Recipients**, choose **Groups**, **Individual students** or **Individual staff**.
6. To send later, set **Schedule for later**.
7. Select **Send now** (or **Schedule message** when a time is set).

A "Message sent" or "Message scheduled" message appears. The checks are "Message body is required" and "Select at least one channel".

> **Important:** Only **In-app** delivery works in this version. The page warns that email, SMS, push and WhatsApp delivery is not connected to a real provider in the demo. Do not rely on those channels for urgent notices unless your school has confirmed they are set up.

Use **History** to see sent and scheduled messages, and **Templates** and **Groups** to prepare reusable text and recipient lists.
