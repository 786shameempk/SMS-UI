When you open the **Dashboard**, an image may appear on top of the page. It is an announcement from School Sphere, such as a new feature or an event. Your school does not need to set anything up for it.

## What to expect

- It appears once each time you sign in to the browser, only on the Dashboard, and never on other pages.
- Select the close button to dismiss it. If there is no close button, it closes by itself after a few seconds.
- If it has a link, select the image to open the link in a new tab. Only secure (https) links open. Selecting the close button never opens the link.
- If the image cannot load, the banner simply does not appear.

## Questions

**Why don't I see it?** There may be no announcement at the moment, or it is not set for your school. Nothing is wrong.

**Can my school turn it off?** School administrators cannot change it. The School Sphere team controls which announcements appear and for which schools.

**Does it appear in the mobile apps?** Yes. The student and parent app, the teacher app and the staff app each show it once on their home screen.
