The **Dashboard** is the first page after you sign in. What it shows depends on your role and the modules you can open.

## Choose a date range

Where the dashboard offers a date range, choose **This year**, **Last 3 months**, **Last 6 months** or **Custom range**.

## Choose which cards you see

1. On the **Dashboard**, select **Manage widgets**.
2. In **Customize your dashboard**, tap a card to show or hide it. Changes apply instantly and are saved to your account or on this device, as the dialog tells you.
3. Use **Hide all**, **Show all** or **Reset to default** to change everything at once. **Reset to default** is available once you have customised the layout.

## Quick actions

The quick actions card links straight to modules you can use, such as Students, Attendance, Fees, Online Classes, Talent Showcase, Announcements, Reports and Homework.

> **Note:** If a card or quick action you expect is missing, your role may not include that module. Ask your administrator; see [Create a role and set its permissions](help:roles-manage).
