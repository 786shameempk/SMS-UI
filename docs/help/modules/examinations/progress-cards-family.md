Open **Progress Cards** in the menu, or **More** → **Progress cards** in the Student app.

- Cards appear only after the school has approved and published them. You also get a notification when a new one is published.
- Cards are grouped by year, newest first. Parents with more than one child choose the child at the top in the app.
- **View** shows the card. **PDF** downloads it; in the app a share sheet opens so you can save, print or send it.
- You only ever see your own children's cards.

> **Note:** If a card looks wrong, ask the school to correct the marks. They regenerate and republish it; the older version stays on file.
