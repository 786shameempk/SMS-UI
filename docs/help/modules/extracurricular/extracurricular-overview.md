**Extra-Curricular** is where the school runs everything outside lessons: sports, music, dance, art, clubs, competitions, houses and green campus programs.

## What you see

The tab bar under the title depends on who you are.

| Tab | What it is for | Who sees it |
|---|---|---|
| Overview | Totals, participation by category, what is coming up, students not in any activity. Parents and students see their own places, houses, teams and awards. | Everyone |
| Activities | The catalog of activities, with places left and a button to join. | Everyone |
| Enrollments | Requests, places and the waiting list. | Everyone |
| Schedule | A week view and an upcoming list of sessions, the register, and an attendance summary. | Everyone (the summary is staff only) |
| Teams | Teams for each activity and their squads. | Staff |
| Houses & groups | Houses, teams, clubs and groups, with shuffling and history. | Staff |
| Events | Sports days, festivals and tournaments, registrations and results. | Everyone |
| Achievements | Medals, trophies and recognition. | Everyone |
| Sustainability | Green campaigns with targets and verified figures. | Staff |
| Settings | Rules and categories. | Staff who can set up the module |

## Who can do what

- **Parents and students** browse open activities, ask to join, withdraw, follow sessions and events, register their own children for events and see their published achievements.
- **Teachers and coaches** see the whole module and can take registers. Whether they can also set things up depends on the permissions the school gave their role.
- **Administrators** set up categories, activities, groups, teams, events and campaigns, approve requests and publish achievements.

> **Note:** The school's subscription plan must include Extra-Curricular, and the role must be allowed to open it in Roles & Permissions. If it is missing from your menu, ask your school administrator.
