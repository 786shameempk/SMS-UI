## Add an activity

1. Open **Extra-Curricular**, select **Activities**, then **New activity**.
2. Fill in **Name** and a short **Code** that is unique this year (for example FB-U14), pick the **Category** and **Academic year**.
3. Choose whether it is **Individual** or **Team**, indoor or outdoor, and its **Status**. Only **Active** activities can be joined; leave it **Draft** while you prepare.
4. Set **Capacity** (empty means no limit), **Minimum to run**, and the **Lowest grade** and **Highest grade** that may join.
5. Optionally add the **Coordinator**, **Venue**, when it meets, equipment, safety instructions and the **Enrollment opens** and **Enrollment closes** dates.
6. Turn **Needs approval** and **Needs parental consent** on or off, then select **Create activity**.

You can lower the capacity only down to the number of students already enrolled.

## Edit or remove

Use the pencil to edit. The bin deletes an activity that was never used; one with enrollments, teams or sessions is set to **Inactive** instead so its history stays readable.

## Categories

The school starts with fifteen categories (sports, music, dance, art, clubs, environment and more). Open **Settings** to add your own, rename, hide, reorder with the arrows, or delete one that has no activities.
