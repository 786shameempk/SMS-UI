## Rules

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| Most activities per student | 0 means no limit. Counts live requests and places. |
| Parents and students can ask to join | Off means only staff enroll students. |
| Stop clashing sessions | An approval is refused if the student would be in two sessions at once. |
| One house or group of each kind per student | Moving a student replaces their old house instead of adding a second. |

Select **Save rules** to apply them.

## Categories

Add, rename, recolour, hide, reorder and delete categories. A category that has activities cannot be deleted; hide it instead. Mark one as **Environmental / green** to group sustainability work.
