## Start a campaign

1. Open **Extra-Curricular**, then **Sustainability**, and select **New campaign**.
2. Enter a **Name**, the kind of programme, **What is measured**, the **Unit** and the **Target**. Add the **Starting value** if there is one.
3. Select **Create campaign**.

## Record figures

1. Select **Figures** on a campaign.
2. Enter the **Date** and **Value**, and say whether it is **Measured** (counted or weighed) or **Estimated** (calculated).
3. An estimate must say **How it was calculated**.
4. Add **Evidence**, participants and volunteer hours, then **Record figure**.

## What counts

The progress bar counts only figures that are **Measured** and **Verified**. Someone with approval rights selects **Verify** on a measured figure that has evidence. Estimates are shown separately and are never counted towards the target. Editing a figure clears its verification.
