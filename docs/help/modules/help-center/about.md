School Sphere is a school management system that runs in a web browser and in three mobile apps. This guide explains how to do everyday tasks, one module at a time, with the exact menu names and buttons you will see.

## How the guide is organised

- **Getting started** covers signing in, passwords and your account.
- **Guides by role** lists the articles written for school administrators, principals, teachers, accountants, librarians, receptionists, parents and students.
- **Modules** follow the menu on the left of the application: Academics, Online Exams, Human Resources, Finance, Campus Operations, Engagement, Insights, and so on. Every article names the menu path to the screen it describes.
- **Troubleshooting** articles list the messages you may see and what to do about them.

Each article is marked with its review state: **Draft** (written, not yet checked), **Checked against the app** (compared with the application's code) or **Verified** (followed step by step in the running application on a stated date).

## What you need

| You need | Details |
|---|---|
| A School Sphere account | Created for you by your school. See [Signing in and your account](help:account-overview). |
| A modern web browser | A current version of a mainstream browser such as Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Mozilla Firefox or Apple Safari. JavaScript must be on. |
| An internet connection | The application and its mobile apps work with your school's online system. |
| For online classes | A device with a camera and microphone, and permission for the browser to use them. |

> **Note:** The exact minimum browser versions are not stated by the application. If something looks wrong, update your browser first.

## Mobile apps

There are three mobile apps: one for **parents and students**, one for **teachers** and one for **staff** such as the librarian, front desk and finance staff. They use the same email address and password as the website, and show only the modules your role allows.

## Your school may differ

Schools choose which modules to switch on and what each role can open under **Roles & Permissions**. If an article describes a screen you cannot see, your school's plan or your role does not include it. Ask your school administrator.
