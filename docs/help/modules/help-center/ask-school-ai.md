Ask School AI can answer "how do I...?" questions from this guide, show the menu path and numbered steps, link to the full article, and take you to the screen when your account is allowed to open it.

## Ask a how-to question

1. Select **Ask School AI** (the floating button, or the one on a Help Center page).
2. Type your question, for example "How do I add a student?".
3. Read the steps. Select **Read the full guide** for the article. When your account can open the screen, a button named after it, such as **Open Students**, takes you there.

## Be taken to a screen

Type something like "Take me to the student list". If your account can open that screen you are taken there; if the question could mean more than one screen, you are asked "Which of these do you mean?" and can pick one; if your role or your school's plan does not include it, you are told that instead.

> **Note:** The assistant never creates, changes or deletes school records on its own for these requests. It only opens screens, and it only opens screens your account is already allowed to open.

## Other questions

Questions that are not about using School Sphere, such as your child's fees or attendance, are answered by the AI assistant from your school's data, as described in [AI Features and Ask School AI](help:ai-overview). A single common word on its own is passed to the assistant rather than treated as a request for a guide.

## When it cannot answer

If the guide has nothing on your question, the assistant says it cannot verify an answer from the documentation rather than guessing. Search the Help Center or ask your school administrator.
