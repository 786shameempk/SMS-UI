## Open the Help Center

- When you are signed in, select the **Help** (question mark) button at the top right and choose **Help Center**.
- You can also go straight to the **/help** page of your school's School Sphere address. The same pages open at **/guide**.
- You do not need to sign in to read the public articles.

## Find an article

1. Type what you want to do in the search box, for example "add a student" or "reset password". Results appear as you type. The words do not have to match exactly: "register" finds "add".
2. Select a result.

If nothing matches, the page suggests trying different words, browsing by module or asking School AI.

## Browse instead of searching

- **Common tasks** lists frequently used how-to articles. Signed-in users see the ones written for their role first.
- **Browse by module** groups modules the way the menu does. Each module page shows its menu path, who gets it by default, and its articles split into overview, how-to, reference, questions and troubleshooting.
- **Browse by role** lists the articles written for one role.
- **Recently viewed** (on this browser only) brings back the articles you opened last.

## Read an article

- The **breadcrumb** at the top shows where you are. **On this page** (on wide screens) jumps to a section.
- Numbered **steps** are in the order you do them. Select a screenshot to enlarge it.
- **Open this screen** takes you to the screen the article describes, when your account can open it. If it cannot, the article says why instead.
- **Related articles** and **Previous / Next** move to neighbouring articles.
- **Was this helpful?** records your answer on this browser.
- **Print this article** prints it without the page controls.

## Download the PDF manual

Select **Download the PDF manual** at the top of the Help Center. The manual is built from the same articles, so it matches what you read online, and its contents list and index show page numbers.
