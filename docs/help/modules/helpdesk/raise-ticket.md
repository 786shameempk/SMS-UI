Use this to record a complaint or support request.

## Steps

1. Open **Help Desk** from the menu. The page is headed **Complaint / Help Desk**.
2. Open the **Raise a Ticket** tab. The card is titled **Raise a ticket**.
3. Choose the **Category** and the **Priority**: **Urgent**, **High**, **Medium** or **Low**.
4. Enter a **Subject** (a short summary) and a **Description** (what happened, and any details).
5. Under **Raised by**, choose who is raising it: student, parent, staff or anonymous. Then pick the student or staff member, or enter the **Parent/guardian name** and an optional **Contact**.
6. Select **Raise ticket**.

A message such as "Ticket … raised" shows the new ticket number. The checks are "Subject is required" and "Description is required".

Track and update tickets on the **Tickets** tab; **Reports** summarises them.

## Reports

The **Reports** tab shows **Overdue tickets** (open or in-progress tickets past their priority's SLA window), **Tickets by category** and **Tickets by priority**.
