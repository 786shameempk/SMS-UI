Approval is switched on in **Settings** (**Lesson plans need approval**). With it off, a submitted plan is published straight away.

## Review

1. Open **Lesson & Portions**, then **Approvals**. **Waiting for review** lists submitted plans and plans a teacher revised after approval, with their topics and history.
2. Select **Approve**, **Ask for changes** or **Reject**. Asking for changes and rejecting need a comment, which the teacher is sent; approving takes an optional note.

You cannot review your own plan, and only submitted plans can be reviewed. The **Decided** tab lists approved, rejected and returned plans.

> **Note:** Every decision, with who made it and when, is kept in the plan's history and the audit trail.
