## Lesson calendar

Open **Lesson & Portions** and select the calendar.

1. Switch between **Month** and **Week**, and use **Previous**, **Next** and **Today** to move around.
2. Lessons appear on the dates set in your [lesson plans](help:lesson-portions-plan-lessons). Holidays and examination days from the school calendar are marked.
3. If a day has too many lessons, **+n more** opens the rest.
4. To move a lesson, open **Move lesson**, pick the **New date**, give a **Reason** and save. It is saved as a revision of the plan with your reason.

If the page says **No lessons planned in this period**, nothing is planned in the range you are viewing. The page also shows the plan's dates. Change them from **My plan**.

## Lesson resources

Link textbooks, reference notes, presentations and worksheets to a syllabus, chapter or topic.

1. Open **Resources** under Lesson & Portions and select **Add resource**.
2. Under **Type**, choose **Study material** (a file from this school) or **Web link**.
3. For a study material, upload the file first in [Study Materials](route:study-materials.home) and then pick it here. There is one place for files and one set of access rules. For a web link, paste the address.
4. Enter a **Title** and choose the **Syllabus**. You can narrow it to a **Chapter** or **Topic**.
5. Choose **Visible**: **Staff only**, or **Show to students and parents**. Families see it only when your school turns the family view on and the syllabus is published.
6. Save. Use **Edit** to change it later, or **Remove** to detach it. A file in Study Materials stays there.
