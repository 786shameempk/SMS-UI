1. Open **Lesson & Portions**, then **Exam portions**, and choose an examination (created in **Examinations**).
2. Tick the chapters or topics each subject covers. The list comes from the class's published syllabus, so the exam points at the same chapters and topics rather than copying them.
3. Select **Save portions**, then **Publish** to show them to students and parents (if the school shows exam syllabus to families). **Unpublish** to change them again.

## Have they been taught?

**Have these been taught?** shows, per subject, how many topics have been completed in every section of the class, and lists the ones that have not. Select **Revision plan** for a suggested order for revising what has been taught, on the teaching days left before the exam.

> **Note:** Only people who set up the syllabus, or approve plans, can change or publish exam portions.
