Open **Syllabus Progress** in the menu. Parents with more than one child choose the child at the top.

| You see | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Subject cards | The share of the syllabus covered, chapters completed, the topic being taught now and the next topics. |
| Show chapters | Each chapter and topic with its status, and the homework recorded for it. Resources the school marked for families appear as links. |
| Exam syllabus | The chapters and topics of upcoming exams, once the school has published them. |

This view is read-only. It never shows a teacher's notes, observations, reasons for delays, or how the school rates a subject's progress; a topic that is delayed simply reads as being taught. If the school has not turned the view on, a message says so.

> **Note:** You see only your own children. A student sees only their own class.
