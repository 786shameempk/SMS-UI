**Lesson & Portions** is where a school plans its syllabus class by class and subject by subject, plans the lessons that cover it, records what was actually taught, and sees which subjects are falling behind. It sits under **Academics** in the menu.

## The tabs

| Tab | What it is for | Who sees it |
|---|---|---|
| Overview | **My teaching**: today's lessons, updates still to record, deadlines. **School overview**: completion against plan, classes and subjects behind, term-end forecast. | Everyone with the module |
| My plan | Your lesson plans: create, submit, revise, reschedule. | People who can create lesson plans |
| Daily progress | Your classes and subjects; mark topics started, completed or delayed. | People who can record progress |
| Calendar | Planned lessons by day, with holidays and exam days marked. | Everyone with the module |
| Class tracking | Completion by class and subject, pending chapters, delayed lessons with a suggested action. | Everyone (teachers see only their own classes) |
| Approvals | Lesson plans waiting for review. | People who can approve plans |
| Syllabus | Curricula, syllabus plans, chapters and topics, templates. | Everyone to read; managers to change |
| Exam portions | Which chapters and topics an exam covers. | Managers, approvers and teachers |
| Subject report, Teacher report | Planned against actual, forecasts, teacher-wise progress. | Subject report: everyone. Teacher report: people who see all classes |
| Resources | Links and study materials attached to the syllabus. | Everyone |
| Settings | School rules, your alert choices, what you may do, the audit trail. | Everyone |

Parents and students do not see these tabs. They see **Syllabus Progress** instead; see [Syllabus progress for parents and students](help:lesson-portions-family).

## Who can do what

The school decides in **Roles & Permissions** (Syllabus & Portions). Until a school changes a role: administrators and principals can do everything; teachers can plan lessons and record progress for the classes and subjects they teach. A teacher never sees another teacher's classes.

## What the percentages mean

Completion counts only topics marked **Completed**. The school chooses whether that is measured by planned periods (the default), topics, chapters or topic weights; see [Settings](help:lesson-portions-settings). The tick on a progress bar is where the plan says the subject should be today, from the chapter dates.
