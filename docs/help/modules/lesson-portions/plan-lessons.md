## Create a plan

1. Open **Lesson & Portions**, then **My plan**, and select **New plan**.
2. Choose the class, optionally a section (leave it to cover all sections) and the subject. The plan follows the class's published syllabus.
3. Give it a title and choose the type: weekly, monthly, term-wise or annual. Set when it starts and ends, and how many periods are available.
4. Tick the chapters or topics you will teach. For each topic you can set a date and the periods. Select **Spread across the dates** to place them in order over the teaching days.
5. Select **Save draft**.

The screen compares the periods your topics need with the timetabled periods between the dates, after holidays and exam days from the school calendar. If there is not enough time it says so; move the end date or choose fewer topics.

## Draft with AI

Select **Draft with AI** to have the AI write a lesson outline into the description from the topics you chose. It is only a draft: read it, edit it, and submit it yourself. Nothing is published or recorded as taught until you (and, if your school requires it, a coordinator) approve it.

## Submit

Choose **Submit for approval** from the plan's row menu. If your school requires approval the plan waits for a coordinator; otherwise it is published straight away. A rejected plan or one with changes requested can be edited and submitted again.

## Revise and reschedule

An approved plan is not overwritten. Choose **Revise** (or **Reschedule lessons** to only move dates), say why, and save: the change is kept as a numbered revision with your reason, and goes back for approval if your school requires it. Lessons can also be moved from the **Calendar** by selecting them.

> **Note:** A teacher plans only for classes and subjects they teach. Assigning a plan to another teacher takes the approval permission, and is recorded.
