## Update a topic

1. Open **Lesson & Portions**, then **Daily progress**. Your classes and subjects are listed with how far each syllabus has got; **To record** shows lessons whose planned date has passed with nothing recorded.
2. Open a class. Chapters that are under way are open; select a chapter to open or close it.
3. Select **Record** (or **Update**) on a topic. Choose **In progress**, **Completed** or **Delayed**, the date it was taught (not in the future) and the periods used.
4. For **Delayed**, say why. Optionally add teaching notes, homework, classroom observations and the date of the next lesson.
5. Select **Save**.

Teaching notes and observations stay inside the school. Homework is shown to parents and students only if the school allows it.

## A whole chapter

Select **Mark chapter taught** to update every topic of the chapter that is not completed, using 1 period each. Topics already completed are left as they are, and the whole set is saved together.

## Mistakes

There is one record per topic and section; a second entry is refused. A **Completed** topic cannot be changed by the teacher. A coordinator can use **Correct** (the shield icon) to change it with a reason; the earlier state stays in the history, which anyone can open with the clock icon. If two people save at the same time, the second is told to reload instead of overwriting the first.

> **Tip:** On the Teacher app, **More**, then **Lesson & portions** shows today's lessons and lets you record progress from your phone.
