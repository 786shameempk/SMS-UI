## Module settings

People who set up the syllabus can change these; everyone else sees them read-only.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| Lesson plans need approval | Submitted plans wait for a coordinator. Off: they are published at once. |
| Parents and students can see syllabus progress | Turns on the read-only view. Off by default. |
| Show homework / Show published examination syllabus | What the family view includes. |
| How completion is measured | Planned periods (default), topics, chapters or topic weights. Only completed topics count. |
| Behind when trailing by | How many percentage points under the plan a subject may be before it is marked **Behind**. |
| Missing update after | Days with nothing recorded before a teacher is reminded. |
| Deadline reminder | How many days before a chapter or term deadline an alert is sent. |
| Alerts the school sends | Switch off any of the six kinds of alert for everyone. |

## My alerts

Switch off the alerts you do not want: upcoming chapter deadlines, overdue lesson plans, missing teaching updates, syllabus behind target, revised lesson plans, and approvals and decisions. You are only ever alerted about classes and plans you are responsible for.

## What you can do here

Shows which of the five actions your role has: set up syllabus and settings, create lesson plans, record progress, approve plans and correct progress, and see school-wide reports. Administrators change them in **Roles & Permissions**.

## Audit trail

People who see school-wide reports can list syllabus creation and revision, deadline changes, lesson plan assignment and decisions, progress updates and corrections, exam portion publication and settings changes, with who made each and when.
