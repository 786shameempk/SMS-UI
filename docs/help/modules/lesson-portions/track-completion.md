## Class tracking

**Class tracking** lists every class, section and subject with a published syllabus. Filter by year, term, class, section, subject and teacher.

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Chapters | Completed / started / not started, out of the total. For example 7 completed, 2 in progress and 3 pending of 12 chapters is 58.3% by chapters. |
| Completion | The bar is the actual completion; the tick is where the plan says it should be today. |
| Status | **On track**, **At risk** (not enough timetabled periods left before the deadline), **Behind** (trailing the plan by more than the school's threshold) or **Overdue** (the deadline has passed), or **Complete**. |
| Forecast finish | When the syllabus will be finished if teaching continues at the pace so far. An estimate. |

The other tabs show **Pending chapters** (what is left, oldest deadline first) and **Delayed lessons and actions**, which gives each late subject a suggested action, such as how many topics a week to teach to catch up, or how many extra periods are needed.

## Drilling into a subject

People who see all classes can select a subject to open its class and section, teacher, chapters, pending topics, the same subject in other sections and a suggested catch-up schedule. The schedule spreads the pending topics over the teaching days left, skipping holidays and exam days. It is a suggestion for the teacher to review; it changes no plan or record.

## Reports

**Subject report** averages completion by subject, compares planned with actual (ahead or behind in percentage points) and shows the term-end forecast. **Exam portions not covered** lists topics in the portions of upcoming exams that have not been taught. **Teacher report** shows, for each teacher, average completion, topics completed and periods used in a date range, and the subjects with no recent update. Every table has an **Export** button for a CSV file, which opens in Excel; **Print or save as PDF** prints the page.

> **Note:** Teachers see only their own classes in all of these. School-wide figures need the permission to see all classes.
