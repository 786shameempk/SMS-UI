## Read your notifications

1. Open **Notifications** from the menu, or from the bell in the header. The page is headed **Notification center** and says how many are unread, or "You're all caught up."
2. On a notification, use its action button (the label it carries, or **View**) to open what it is about, **Mark read** to clear it, or the delete button to remove it.
3. Select **Mark all read** to clear every unread notification ("All notifications marked read").

Notifications have a category, such as Announcement, Academic, Finance, Event, System, Alert, Talent Showcase, Online Classes or Messages.
