The **Online Classes** page greets you by name ("Hi …") and has the lists **Today**, **Upcoming**, **Past** and (for organisers) **Drafts**, filtered by kind: **Online class**, **Parent meeting**, **Staff & management**, **Exam** and **Other**. The links **Calendar**, **Attendance**, **Recordings** and **Reports** are at the top. A banner may offer to **Turn on** reminders on this device.

## Join or start

On a class, the host sees **Start class** (or **Start meeting**) and everyone invited sees **Join now** once the room is open. Before then, a disabled button shows when it opens.

The class's page has tabs for **People**, **Attendance**, **Materials**, **Notes** and **Recordings**, and **Activity** for those allowed to see it.

**Calendar** (the Class calendar) shows classes by date, and **Reports** (Online class reports) has **Overview**, **Student attendance** and **Teachers** (and **Settings** for administrators).
