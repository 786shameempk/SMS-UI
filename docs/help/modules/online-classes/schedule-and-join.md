Teachers, principals and administrators see **Schedule class** and **Schedule meeting** on the **Online Classes** page.


1. Open **Online Classes** from the menu and select **Schedule class**. The dialog is titled **Schedule a class** (or an exam, or a meeting, for those types). It says everyone you invite gets a notification and a reminder before it starts.
2. Enter a **Title** ("Give it a title"). Choose the **Class** (required for a class, optional otherwise) and the **Subject**.
3. Pick the **Date** ("Pick a date") or, for a repeating class, the **First class**, then **Starts** ("Pick a start time") and **Length**.
4. To repeat, switch on repeating, choose the days of the week and the **Until** date.
5. Choose **Who's invited**, and **Remind everyone** when you want reminders.
6. Under **Where**, choose **Built-in video room** (attendance is taken for you) or **Paste a link** for Zoom, Meet or Teams. A pasted link stays hidden until someone joins, and only invited people get it.
7. Optionally add a **Description**.
8. Select **Schedule class** (or **Schedule … sessions** for a series), or **Save as draft**.

A message confirms how many sessions were scheduled. If some people have no login yet, you are told they were not invited; link their accounts in their Student or Staff profiles.
