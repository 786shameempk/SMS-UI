## Steps

1. Open **My Exams** in the **Online Exams** menu. **Upcoming Exams**, **Completed Exams** and **My Results** are in the same menu.
2. Find the exam. When it is open, the card has **Start exam**. If you already used an attempt and may try again, it reads **Try again**. If you left one unfinished, it reads **Resume exam**.
3. Answer the questions. A countdown shows the time left, and your answers are saved as you go.
4. Select **Submit** when you are finished. The dialog **Submit your exam?** warns that you cannot change your answers after submitting. Select **Submit exam** to confirm, or **Keep working** to go back. If the exam allows review before submitting, the dialog also lists unanswered or marked questions.

If time runs out, the exam is submitted for you.

Your result appears on **My Results** once it is available. Whether it shows straight away, and whether it includes the correct answers, is decided by the teacher.
