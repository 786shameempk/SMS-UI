The Parent Portal gathers everything about your child's school day in one place. Sign in with the account your school gave you; see [Sign in to School Sphere](help:account-sign-in).

## Choose a child

If more than one child is linked to your account, choose the child with the child switcher at the top. Most tabs show information for that child.

If you see "No children are linked to this account yet", the school has not linked your account to a student. Ask the school office.

## Tabs

**Overview**, **Attendance**, **Homework**, **Exam Results**, **Fees**, **Messages**, **Leave**, **Track bus**, **Notifications** and **Learning profile**. **Ask School AI** appears when the school has it on. The Overview also has quick actions such as **Pay fees**, **Apply for leave**, **Message teacher** and **Track bus**.

## Pay a fee online

1. Open the **Fees** tab.
2. On the invoice you want to pay, select **Pay online**.
3. Check the amount in **Pay … fee**. You will pay on Razorpay's secure page with UPI, card or netbanking; your card and UPI details go only to Razorpay.
4. Select **Continue to pay** with the amount.

"Payment received for …" confirms it, and the receipt appears once the payment is confirmed. If the school has not set up online payment, the dialog says "Online payment isn't set up for this school yet. Please pay at the school office."

## Apply for leave

1. Open the **Leave** tab and select **New request**.
2. Set **From** and **To**, and type a **Reason**. All three are required ("Start date is required", "End date is required", "Reason is required").
3. Select **Submit request** ("Leave request submitted").
