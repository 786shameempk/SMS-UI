The **Report library** tab of **Reports & analytics** is a searchable catalogue of the reports your role may open, gathered from every part of School Sphere. It sits next to the chart tabs described in [Find the report you need](help:reports-overview).

## Find a report

1. Open **Reports & Analytics** and choose the **Report library** tab.
2. Type in **Search reports** (name, category or keyword), or pick a category under **Report categories** (**All categories**). **Frequently used** and **Recently opened** shortcuts appear above the list.
3. Each report shows how many filters and columns it has. A report marked **Restricted** needs a permission you may not have.

If the list is empty you see "No reports are available to you": the school's plan must include Reports & Analytics and your role must be allowed to view them. "No reports match your search" means you should try a different word or another category.

## Run it

1. Select a report. Set the filters shown under **Report filters**, for example **Period** (a date range or a **Month**), then select **Apply filters**. **Reset filters** clears them.
2. The results appear as a table with **Rows per page** and page buttons. Select **Refresh** to reload.

## Export or print

Select **Export** and choose what to include: **Current page** or **All filtered records**, in **CSV** or **Excel (summary and data sheets)**. "Export downloaded." confirms it. **Print** opens a print preview; choose **Save as PDF** there to keep a copy.

> **Note:** Reports respect the same access rules as the rest of School Sphere. You only see records you are allowed to see, and exports contain only what is on screen for the filters you chose.
