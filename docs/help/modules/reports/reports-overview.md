**Reports & analytics** shows live charts and figures pulled from the other modules. Open **Reports & Analytics** from the menu; the page is headed **Reports & analytics**. Choose a tab:

| Tab | Figures at the top | Charts |
|---|---|---|
| **Student Performance** | **Overall average**, **Exams tracked**, **Latest exam** | **Average score by exam** (school-wide, in date order) and **Average by class** for the latest exam |
| **Attendance** | **Student attendance (avg.)**, **Staff attendance (avg.)** | **Attendance trend**: student against staff percentage over the last 6 months |
| **Fee Collection** | **Collected**, **Pending**, **Overdue**, **Collection rate** | **Fee collection**: collected (by payment date) against pending (by due date) over the last 6 months |
| **Teacher Performance** | **Teachers with classes**, **School-wide average** | **Average class performance by teacher** (top 10) |
| **Admissions** | **Total applications**, **Enrollment rate**, **Enrolled** | **Applications by status** and **Applications received** over the last 6 months |
| **Dropout Analysis** | **Total students**, **Inactive (dropout)**, **Dropout rate** | **Students by status** and **Inactive students by class** |
| **Library Usage** | **Total loans**, **Currently issued**, **Overdue** | **Loan volume** per month for 6 months and **Busiest categories** |
| **Transport** | **Students assigned**, **Total seating capacity**, **Overall utilization** | **Route utilization**: active assignments as a share of each route's bus capacity |
| **Hostel Occupancy** | **Beds occupied**, **Total beds**, **Overall occupancy** | **Occupancy by hostel**: occupied against vacant beds |

> **Note:** You see only the reports your role may open. Some figures, such as the fee totals, are sensitive; share them only with people who should see them.
