Two tabs on the **Settings** page control how the school's portal looks and reads: **Appearance** and **Localization**.

## Before you start

- You need access to **Settings** (school administrators have it).
- Open **Administration** > **Settings**.

## Change the appearance

1. Open the **Appearance** tab.
2. Under **Theme mode**, choose **Light** or **Dark**. This is your own preference on this device. It is not shared with the rest of the school.
3. Under **Brand theme**, select a colour such as **Green**, **Ocean Blue**, **Royal Plum** or **Slate & Saffron**. The sidebar, buttons and badges change straight away for the whole school.
4. Under **Corner style**, choose **Sharp**, **Rounded** or **Pill**. This sets how rounded buttons, cards and inputs look.
5. Under **Density**, choose **Comfortable** or **Compact**. **Compact** packs cards, tables and lists more tightly.

Each choice saves as soon as you select it, and a message confirms the change.

> **Note:** Brand theme, Corner style and Density apply to everyone in the school. Only Theme mode is personal.

## Set language, timezone, currency and date format

1. Open the **Localization** tab.
2. Choose the **Language**. Options include English (India), English (US), Malayalam, Hindi, Tamil and Arabic. This is the default interface language for people who have not chosen their own.
3. Choose the **Timezone**, for example India Standard Time.
4. Choose the **Currency**: INR, USD, GBP or AED.
5. Choose the **Date format**: `DD/MM/YYYY`, `MM/DD/YYYY` or `YYYY-MM-DD`. The line **Preview:** shows today's date in that format.
6. Select **Save settings**.

> **Note:** The currency is stored as the school's preference. Screens that already show amounts keep showing rupees (₹).
