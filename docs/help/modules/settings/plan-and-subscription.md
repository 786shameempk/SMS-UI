The **Plan & Subscription** tab shows what your school has bought and how much of it you are using. It is read-only: renewals and plan changes are arranged with your School Sphere account team.

## Read the tab

1. Open **Administration** > **Settings** and select **Plan & Subscription**.
2. **Current Plan** shows the plan name and its status: **Active**, **Trial**, **Suspended** or **Cancelled**. A note beside the status says what that means for access.
3. The details show **Subscribed since**, and either **Trial ends** or **Current term ends** with the number of days left. **Renewal** and **Billing contact** are listed too.
4. **Usage** shows **Students** and **Staff** counted against the plan across every branch, and **Document storage**. A meter turns to a warning, **Nearing your plan's limit.**, at 80% or more. **No limit on your plan.** means there is no cap.
5. **Included modules** lists the modules enabled for your school. [Roles & Permissions](help:roles-manage) decides who can use each one.
6. **Enabled features** lists what the plan includes, and **Available additional modules** lists modules offered on other plans.

## Ask for more

Select **Contact Administrator** at the bottom of the tab to ask the team about extra modules or a different plan.

> **Note:** If the subscription is **Suspended** or **Cancelled**, contact the School Sphere team to restore access.
