Open **Staff Management**, choose **View profile** on the person's row, then the tab you need. Teachers have a similar profile under **Teachers**.

## Qualifications & Experience

- Under **Qualifications**, select **Add**. Enter the **Degree**, **Institution** and **Year completed** (all required), then **Add** ("Qualification added").
- Under **Experience**, select **Add**. Enter the **Organization**, **Role** and **From year**; **To year** and **Description** are optional. Then **Add** ("Experience added").
- Each entry has a remove button ("Qualification removed", "Experience removed").

## Salary

- Under **Salary structure**, enter the **Basic**, **Allowances** and **Deductions**, and the **Bank name** and **Account number**. Select **Save salary structure** ("Salary structure saved"). The button stays off until you change something.
- **Payment history** lists payments. The button **Record this month's payment** records it ("Payment recorded"); once paid it reads **Paid this month**. For a whole month at once, use [Run monthly payroll](help:payroll-run).

## Performance

Under **Performance reviews**, select **Add review**. Enter the **Reviewer**, a **Rating** and **Comments** (the reviewer and comments are required), then **Add review** ("Performance review added").

## Attendance and Documents

The **Attendance** tab shows the person's attendance. On **Documents**, select to upload a file ("Document uploaded"); the remove button deletes it ("Document removed").
