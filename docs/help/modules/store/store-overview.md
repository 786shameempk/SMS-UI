**Store Management** is a main item in the sidebar, below Co-Curricular. It runs the school's stores and warehouses: what the school owns, what it buys, what it hands out and what it sells.

## What you see

A menu down the left of the page (a **Menu** button at the top on phones) shows only what you are allowed to open, in five groups:

- **Overview**: Dashboard.
- **Master data**: Stores & Warehouses, Items, Categories, Suppliers.
- **Purchasing**: Purchase Requisitions, Purchase Orders, Goods Receipt (GRN).
- **Stock and distribution**: Stock Management, Item Issue / Distribution, Returns.
- **Sales and insight**: Store Sales, Reports.

A number beside an entry is how much is waiting there (requisitions or receipts to approve, item and return requests, low-stock items). The page you are on is highlighted. The bell beside the title lists alerts for you, such as low stock or a request waiting for approval.

## Rules the module keeps

- Stock goes **up** only when a goods receipt is recorded (and approved, if the school asks for that), when opening stock or an inward adjustment is entered, or when a return is approved.
- Stock goes **down** only through an issue, a sale, a supplier return, a transfer, or an adjustment (damaged, expired, lost).
- You can never issue or sell more than a store holds, and two people cannot take the last unit at the same time.
- Every change is a line in the **stock ledger** that cannot be edited or removed. A correction is a new line.
- Every document has its own number, such as **PO-2026-00012**.
- Items, categories and suppliers are shared by the whole school; stores and their stock belong to one campus.

## Ask School AI

Staff who can view the store can ask **Ask School AI** things like "how much stock do we have", "what is running low" or "how many notebooks are left". It only reads, and answers with what that person is allowed to see.

## Hiding tabs for a role

A school can hide individual menu entries for a role under **Roles & Permissions > Sub-menus > Store Management**. This only tidies the screen; what a role may do is still set by its permissions.
