## Create a survey

1. Open **Surveys & Feedback** from the menu and stay on the **Surveys** tab.
2. Select **New survey**.
3. Enter a **Title** and, if you like, a **Description**.
4. Choose the **Audience**: **Students**, **Parents**, **Staff** or **Everyone**. **Created by** is optional.
5. Set when it **Opens** (required) and optionally when it **Closes**.
6. Select **Add question** and write each question. Choose its type: **Rating (1–5)**, **Multiple choice**, **Yes / No** or **Open text**, and whether it is required. Multiple choice questions need options.
7. Select **Save as draft**.

A message says the survey was saved as a draft. The checks are "Title is required", "Open date is required", "Question text is required" and "Add at least one question".

## Publish, close or delete

On the survey's row, open the actions menu. **Publish** is offered for a draft ("Survey published"), **Close** for a published survey ("Survey closed"), **View results** shows the results, and **Delete** removes it ("Survey deleted").

## Record a response

1. Open the **Record a Response** tab and choose the **Survey**.
2. Choose the **Respondent**: **A student**, **A parent / guardian** or **A staff member**, then pick the person (for a parent, enter the **Parent/guardian name** and optionally the **Child**).
3. Answer the questions and select **Submit response** ("Response recorded").

## Reports

The **Reports** tab shows **Responses by audience**, which groups are engaging most with surveys.
