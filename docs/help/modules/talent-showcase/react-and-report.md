## Browse

Open **Talent Showcase** from the menu. The **Discover** page has rails such as **Featured talents**, **Trending now**, **From your school**, **Recently added**, **Most appreciated**, **Most viewed** and **Teacher talents**. Open any card to see the showcase. A creator's page lists their **Talent highlights** and **All published showcases**; a school's page shows **Featured talents**, **Our talents by category**, **Student talents** and **Teacher talents**.

## React

On a published showcase, the **Appreciate this talent** group offers the reactions **Like**, **Love**, **Appreciate**, **Amazing** and **Congratulations**. Reactions open only once the work is published.

## Report

If a showcase should not be shared, select **Report content** on its page. If you have already reported it, the page says "You've reported this." **Report this showcase** asks why:

- **Inappropriate content**
- **Bullying or harassment**
- **Privacy concern**
- **Not their own work**
- **Spam or misleading**
- **Something else**, where you add details

After you send it you see "Thanks - the school's moderators will take a look." Moderators handle it from the **Reports** tab of the [Review center](help:talent-review).

If you open a showcase that is private, still under review or removed, the page says "This showcase isn't available" with **Back to Discover**.
