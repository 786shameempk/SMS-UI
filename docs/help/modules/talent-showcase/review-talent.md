Only teachers and school administrators can use the **Review center**; other people see "Reviewers only".

## Review a submission

1. Open **Talent Showcase**, then the **Review center**. On the **Approval queue**, open a submission, or choose **Review this submission** on a talent's page.
2. Read the work. The panel shows its category and the visibility that was requested.
3. Choose **Approve**, **Request changes** or **Decline**.
4. Write a comment. It is optional for approval; for the other two, be kind and specific about what should change.
5. Select the button, which is named after your choice: **Approve & publish**, **Send back for changes** or **Decline submission**.

A message confirms it: "Approved and published", "Changes requested - the creator has been notified" or "Declined - the creator has been notified". The **Reviewed** tab lists past decisions.

## Manage a talent

On a talent's page, people with the right access can **Edit**, **Restore to drafts**, **Feature this talent** (or **Remove from featured**), **Share link** ("Link copied") and **Archive**. The creator can **Submit for approval** or **Resubmit for approval**.

## Reports and settings (administrators)

- **Reports** lists work that people flagged. Resolve a report by dismissing it ("Report dismissed") or hiding the content ("Content hidden").
- **Settings** has the **Tagline** and the **Approval rules**: **Who reviews student submissions?** is either **Teachers & school admins** or **School admins only**. Save with a "Settings saved" message.
