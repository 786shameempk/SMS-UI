## Share a talent

1. Open **Talent Showcase** from the menu and select **Share your talent**.
2. Under **Pick a category**, choose the kind of talent.
3. Enter a **Title** (under 150 characters) and **Tell the story**: what inspired it and what you are proud of.
4. Add up to 8 **Tags** to help others discover it.
5. **Add your media**: a photo, video or recording. For written work, write at least 20 characters in the description instead.
6. Under **Who can see it once approved?**, choose who may see it: **School Only** or **Public**. The public option is offered only if your school allows it.
7. Select **Save draft** to keep working, or **Submit for approval**. For something already submitted, the button reads **Resubmit for approval**.

A message says "Draft saved" or "Submitted! We'll notify you once it's reviewed."

The checks include "Give your showcase a title" and "Keep the title under 150 characters".

## Change a showcase

On your own showcase's page, **Edit** opens the same form with your work filled in, so you can change it and use **Save draft** or **Submit for approval** (**Resubmit for approval** once it has been submitted). If a reviewer asks for changes, edit it and resubmit.

## Review submissions (teachers and school administrators)

Open the **Review center**. The **Approval queue** shows how many are waiting and how many are from students. **Reviewed** shows past decisions. Administrators also see **Reports** (flagged work) and **Settings**.

Other pages: **Explore talents** to browse by category, **My talents** for your own, and creator and school pages.

## Find and follow your own work

- **Explore talents** lets you browse by category. Search with **Search talents** (for example a talent, a name or a school) and use the scope buttons **All schools** and **My school**.
- **My talents** lists your own showcases. Filter them with **All**, **Drafts**, **Pending**, **Published**, **Needs changes** and **Archived**; the counts show how many are in each.
