Use this when the school tracks buses with **Driver's phone**.

## Before you start

- You need a School Sphere login with the **Drive a bus trip** permission, linked to your staff record, and a bus assigned to you. Otherwise the page says "No bus is assigned to you" and asks you to contact the school office.
- Your device must be able to share location. If not, the page says "This device or browser cannot share location."

## Steps

1. Open **Transport** and select the **Driver trip** tab.
2. Choose your bus in **Select your bus**.
3. Allow location access when your phone asks.
4. Select **Start trip**. A **Sharing location** badge appears.
5. Keep the page open and the screen on while you drive.
6. When you finish, select **End trip**.

> **Note:** If the school is not using driver phones for tracking, the page says parents will not see this location. An administrator can change that under **Tracking setup**.
