About the founder

Fourteen years of building software for others. Now building our own.

I am Shameem P.K., a software professional with 14 years of experience in the software industry. I have worked with international clients and helped develop and deliver software across many domains.

SMS-SchoolSphere is my first step toward building my own software company and product business.

My vision is a technology company that makes practical, scalable and affordable software for real business problems. We are starting with education, where there is a clear opportunity to simplify and modernise how schools are run.

Why SMS-SchoolSphere

One platform for the whole school

SMS-SchoolSphere is a modern, cloud-based school management platform that brings administrators, teachers, students and parents together in one place.

Our focus is more than the software. We are building a sustainable SaaS business with real customers, recurring revenue and long-term growth.

Phase 1: Build & Stabilize

MONTHS 1–3
  • Register the company and set up its legal and business structure.
  • Finalise and stabilise the SMS-SchoolSphere platform.
  • Strengthen security, scalability, reliability and user experience.
  • Complete the production infrastructure.
  • Start pilots with selected schools.

Phase 2: Validate & Acquire Customers

MONTHS 4–9
  • Begin focused sales and marketing to schools.
  • Onboard the first schools and paying customers.
  • Set up a structured onboarding and support process.
  • Build a repeatable sales and implementation model.
  • Develop customer references and case studies.
  • Keep improving the product from real-world use.
MilestoneShow product-market fit and a repeatable way to win customers.

Phase 3: Scale SchoolSphere

MONTHS 10–18
  • Expand across schools in Kerala.
  • Gradually enter other Indian markets.
  • Grow recurring SaaS revenue.
  • Add high-demand modules and integrations.
  • Grow the sales, customer success, support and product teams.
  • Scale infrastructure for a much larger user base.

Long-term vision

Education first, then beyond

Focusing on one sector first lets us build a strong product, understand customers deeply, earn recurring revenue and create an operating model that scales.

Once SMS-SchoolSphere reaches meaningful milestones in adoption, revenue, stability and operations, we will use the technology and experience we have built to explore sectors beyond education.
