Problems it solves

Less paperwork. Fewer phone calls. Clearer answers.

Most schools run on registers, spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups and separate fee software. SchoolSphere replaces that patchwork with one system everyone shares.

✕Student records spread across files, registers and spreadsheets.
✓One profile per student: admission, guardians, documents, attendance, marks and fees.
✕Attendance taken on paper, then typed up again at the end of the month.
✓Teachers mark a class in a couple of taps, even offline, and reports build themselves.
✕Fee collection tracked by hand, with overdue amounts found too late.
✓Fee structures, invoices, online payments, receipts and overdue tracking in one place.
✕Parents call the office to ask about homework, attendance and fees.
✓Parents see it all in the app, get notifications and can ask School AI.
✕Teachers spend free periods writing question papers and report remarks.
✓AI drafts papers, worksheets and remarks in minutes; teachers review and approve.
✕Management has no live view of how the school is doing.
✓Live dashboards and ready-made reports across attendance, academics and fees.
